Teaching

Instructor of Record at UC Irvine, Brandeis University, and Cal Poly Pomona

Whether teaching introductory surveys or advanced seminars, my teaching centers on building critical thinking skills and a healthy academic community that is responsive to students of diverse backgrounds. I draw on flipped classroom methods, free course materials, and student-centered design to make political science accessible to non-majors, international students, and first-generation scholars alike. I have four courses as instructor of record and five years of Teaching Assistant experience at UC Irvine.
